## **6 Mechanical Information**

![](hardware/resources/unit_top_v_1_0_0_i2s_ics43434_mems_microphone.png){width=5.4in}

The board provides two mounting holes, seven edge connections, and a
right-angle JST-SH connector. A controlled dimensional drawing has not yet
been added, so do not scale dimensions from the rendered image.

Allow clearance beneath the ICS-43434 bottom acoustic port. Do not cover the
port with a flat enclosure wall, adhesive, solder mask contamination, or
mounting hardware.
